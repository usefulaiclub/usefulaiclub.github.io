Useful AI Club Control Room

Control Room is the owner’s private publishing tool for the owner’s two channels: Useful AI Club (@usefulaiclub) and Notes Decoded (@notes.decoded on TikTok), a perfume channel. It runs on the owner’s own server and is reachable only on the owner’s private network. No one else can sign up or sign in.

  • Review. The owner watches each finished video and approves it or sends it back.
  • Schedule. An approved video goes to the channel’s next posting time on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. For TikTok the owner fills in TikTok’s post form for each video and confirms it.
  • Post to TikTok. The owner connects each channel’s TikTok account once with Login Kit. At the slot, Control Room uploads the video with TikTok’s Content Posting API, using the caption, privacy and interaction settings the owner chose on TikTok’s post form, then shows the post’s status.
  • Track. It shows each post’s status and link, and each channel’s own YouTube numbers.

Open Control Room (owner only: it opens only on the owner’s private network).

It uses TikTok’s user.info.basic and video.publish permissions only. See the Privacy Policy for what it reads and stores, and how to disconnect.

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026. Covers this website and the app Useful AI Club Control Room.

This website

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It has no tracking and no analytics.
  • It loads nothing from third parties.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

The app

Useful AI Club Control Room is private. It runs on the owner’s own server, is reachable only on the owner’s private network, and handles only the accounts of the owner’s own two channels, Useful AI Club and Notes Decoded. No one else can sign up or sign in.

TikTok

The app asks TikTok for two permissions: user.info.basic and video.publish. With them it uses only:

  • The account’s open ID, username and display name, to show which account a video will go to and to check it is the channel’s own account (@usefulaiclub or @notes.decoded).
  • The account’s posting settings, fetched fresh each time the post form opens: the privacy choices it allows, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are turned off, and the longest video it may post.
  • The status of each video the owner posts, and its TikTok post ID once it is public, to show “Posted” and link to it.

The app does not read followers, other users, messages, or the account’s other videos. It posts only a video the owner has approved, with the caption and settings the owner chose and confirmed on TikTok’s post form.

Instagram

  • Account ID and username, to check it is the channel’s own account.
  • Publishing the channel’s own Reels.

YouTube

  • Channel ID.
  • Analytics for the channel’s own videos: views, watch time, average view duration, likes, comments and shares.
  • Uploading the channel’s own videos and posting a comment on them.

Facebook

  • The Page ID and a Page access token the owner puts in the app’s settings.
  • Publishing the channel’s own Reels to its own Page.

Nothing is posted unless the owner approves the video. A TikTok post also needs the owner’s confirmation on TikTok’s post form, every time.

What is stored

  • For each platform the app stores only the sign-in tokens that platform gives it (for TikTok: the refresh token and the account’s open ID; the short-lived access token is kept in memory only), in a private file on the owner’s server.
  • Tokens are never sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them, and never shown in a browser.
  • The app keeps a local record of what it posted and when, with each post’s ID.
  • Data is not sold, not shared, not used for ads and not used for profiling.
  • So that Instagram can fetch a booked Reel, one copy of that video is reachable at a random, unguessable link from about 20 minutes before its slot until it is published (at most 2 hours after the slot). The copy is then deleted.

Disconnect and delete

  • TikTok: in the TikTok app go to Profile, Menu, Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions, choose Useful AI Club Control Room and tap Remove access. The stored tokens stop working at once.
  • Instagram and Facebook: remove the app in Instagram’s or Meta’s apps and websites settings.
  • YouTube: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/connections.

To ask for the local tokens and records to be deleted, email the address below. They are deleted within 30 days.
